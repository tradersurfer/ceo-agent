# BCG Growth-Share Matrix Framework

## Definition
Classify a portfolio by market growth and relative market share as Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, or Dogs.

## When to Use
Resource allocation across a portfolio.

## Execution Steps
1. Calculate relative market share and market growth rate for each product/SBU.
2. Plot items into 4 quadrants:
   - **Stars:** High Growth, High Share (Invest for growth).
   - **Cash Cows:** Low Growth, High Share (Harvest cash).
   - **Question Marks:** High Growth, Low Share (Evaluate/Fund or Divest).
   - **Dogs:** Low Growth, Low Share (Liquidate or Divest).
3. Allocate corporate budget according to matrix priorities.

## Expected Output
Investment and divestment priorities.
