# SWOT + TOWS Framework

## Definition
Compare internal strengths and weaknesses with external opportunities and threats, then convert the diagnosis into actionable TOWS strategies.

## When to Use
Situational diagnosis or strategy formulation.

## Execution Steps
1. Audit internal Strengths (S) and Weaknesses (W).
2. Audit external Opportunities (O) and Threats (T).
3. **SO Strategies (Maxi-Maxi):** Use strengths to capitalize on opportunities.
4. **WO Strategies (Mini-Maxi):** Overcome weaknesses by exploiting opportunities.
5. **ST Strategies (Maxi-Mini):** Use strengths to mitigate external threats.
6. **WT Strategies (Mini-Mini):** Minimize weaknesses and avoid threats.

## Expected Output
Prioritized strategic options.
