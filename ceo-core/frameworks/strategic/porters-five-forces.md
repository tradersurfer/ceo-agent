# Porter's Five Forces Framework

## Definition
Assess industry structure through rivalry, new entrants, substitutes, buyer power, and supplier power.

## When to Use
Industry attractiveness, competitive strategy, or market-entry analysis.

## Execution Steps
1. **Threat of New Entrants:** Evaluate capital requirements, economies of scale, and regulatory barriers.
2. **Bargaining Power of Buyers:** Evaluate buyer concentration, switching costs, and price sensitivity.
3. **Bargaining Power of Suppliers:** Evaluate supplier concentration, availability of substitutes, and unique inputs.
4. **Threat of Substitutes:** Analyze relative price-performance ratio of alternative solutions.
5. **Competitive Rivalry:** Assess industry growth rate, exit barriers, and competitor concentration.

## Expected Output
An attractiveness score with strategic implications.
