# Balanced Scorecard + Strategy Maps Framework

## Definition
Connect objectives and metrics across Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning and Growth perspectives.

## When to Use
Strategy execution or performance management.

## Execution Steps
1. **Financial Perspective:** Define top-line and return objectives.
2. **Customer Perspective:** Define value propositions for target buyers.
3. **Internal Process Perspective:** Identify key operations to satisfy customer goals.
4. **Learning & Growth Perspective:** Identify required skills, culture, and technology infrastructure.
5. Connect perspectives via cause-and-effect lines on a Strategy Map.

## Expected Output
Cascaded objectives with leading and lagging indicators.
