# Galbraith's Star Model Framework

## Definition
Align five organizational design levers — Strategy, Structure, Processes, Rewards, and People — so that each reinforces the others rather than working against them.

## When to Use
Organization design, restructuring, or diagnosing why structure and incentives pull against strategy.

## Execution Steps
1. Verify **Strategy** direction.
2. Align **Structure** (org chart, decision rights).
3. Align **Processes** (information flow and workflows).
4. Align **Rewards** (incentives and performance management).
5. Align **People** (hiring, training, mindsets).

## Expected Output
A five-lever alignment diagnosis identifying which design levers to change.
