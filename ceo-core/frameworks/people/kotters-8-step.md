# Kotter's 8-Step Change Model Framework

## Definition
Lead large-scale change through eight steps, from establishing urgency to anchoring new approaches in the culture.

## When to Use
Planning and sequencing a major organizational change.

## Execution Steps
1. Create Urgency.
2. Build a Guiding Coalition.
3. Form a Strategic Vision.
4. Enlist a Volunteer Army.
5. Enable Action by Removing Barriers.
6. Generate Short-Term Wins.
7. Sustain Acceleration.
8. Institute Change into Culture.

## Expected Output
A staged change plan identifying the current step and next actions.
