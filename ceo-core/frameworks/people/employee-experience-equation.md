# Employee Experience (EX) Equation Framework

## Definition
Frame employee experience as the gap between employee expectations and the reality the organization delivers across the work, the people, and the environment.

## When to Use
Diagnosing engagement, attrition, or experience gaps.

## Execution Steps
1. Benchmark employee expectations across Culture, Tech Environment, and Physical Space.
2. Measure actual operational delivery across touchpoints.
3. Identify expectation-reality gaps driving attrition or disengagement.

## Expected Output
An expectation-versus-reality gap analysis with prioritized interventions.
