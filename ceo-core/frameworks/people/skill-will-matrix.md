# Skill-Will Matrix Framework

## Definition
Match a person's skill and will for a specific task to the right leadership response — direct, guide, excite, or delegate.

## When to Use
Choosing a delegation or coaching approach, or diagnosing task-level performance.

## Execution Steps
1. Assess task-level Skill (Low/High) and Will (Low/High).
2. Apply leadership response:
   - **Low Skill, High Will:** Guide / Train.
   - **High Skill, High Will:** Delegate.
   - **Low Skill, Low Will:** Direct.
   - **High Skill, Low Will:** Excite / Motivate.

## Expected Output
A quadrant placement with the matched management response.
