# ADKAR Model Framework

## Overview
Diagnose individual and team change readiness across five sequential building blocks: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, and Reinforcement.

## When to Use
Diagnosing where an individual or group is stuck in adopting an organizational or technological change.

## Execution Steps
1. **Awareness:** Verify if the team understands the business reasons for the change.
2. **Desire:** Evaluate individual willingness and support factors.
3. **Knowledge:** Assess training and skill preparation.
4. **Ability:** Verify capability to execute the new behavior in daily operations.
5. **Reinforcement:** Identify incentives and governance mechanisms to prevent backsliding.

## Expected Output Schema
- **Blocking Stage:** Primary ADKAR bottleneck identified (e.g., Knowledge gap).
- **Targeted Intervention:** Action plan to resolve the specific blockage.
