# Spans and Layers Framework

## Definition
Analyze managerial spans of control and the number of reporting layers between the top and the front line.

## When to Use
Diagnosing organizational drag, management cost, or slow decision flow.

## Execution Steps
1. Map current reporting depth (Layers between CEO and frontline).
2. Calculate average direct reports per manager (Span of Control).
3. Identify management bloat or overly wide spans causing bottlenecks.
4. Recommend delayering or span optimization targets.

## Expected Output
A spans-and-layers profile with delayering or re-span recommendations.
