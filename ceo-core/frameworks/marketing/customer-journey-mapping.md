# Customer Journey Mapping Framework

## Definition
Visualize the end-to-end customer experience across interactions and stages.

## When to Use
Experience design.

## Execution Steps
1. Define buyer persona and target goals.
2. Map stages: Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Onboarding, Retention, Advocacy.
3. Document actions, touchpoints, thoughts, and emotions at every stage.
4. Identify friction points and operational improvement opportunities.

## Expected Output
A journey map with prioritized friction points.
