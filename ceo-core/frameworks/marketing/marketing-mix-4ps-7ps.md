# 4Ps / 7Ps Marketing Mix Framework

## Definition
Coordinate Product, Price, Place, and Promotion, plus People, Process, and Physical Evidence where relevant.

## When to Use
Tactical marketing planning.

## Execution Steps
1. **Product:** Define features, packaging, branding, and core value.
2. **Price:** Set pricing strategy (Skimming, Penetration, Value-based).
3. **Place:** Select distribution channels and retail touchpoints.
4. **Promotion:** Design advertising, PR, content, and direct sales strategy.
5. *(Extended 7Ps for Services)* **People**, **Process**, **Physical Evidence**.

## Expected Output
A coherent marketing mix.
