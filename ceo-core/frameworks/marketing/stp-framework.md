# STP Framework

## Definition
Develop market strategy through Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning.

## When to Use
Market strategy or go-to-market planning.

## Execution Steps
1. **Segmentation:** Divide market into distinct groups (Geographic, Demographic, Psychographic, Behavioral).
2. **Targeting:** Evaluate segment attractiveness and select target segments.
3. **Positioning:** Define unique value proposition and brand positioning for selected segments.

## Expected Output
Chosen segments with a positioning statement.
