# CLV + CAC Framework

## Definition
Compare customer lifetime value with customer acquisition cost.

## When to Use
Growth economics or retention investment.

## Execution Steps
1. Compute CAC ($\text{Total Sales & Marketing Spend} / \text{New Customers Acquired}$).
2. Compute Customer Lifetime Value ($\text{Average Order Value} \times \text{Purchase Frequency} \times \text{Gross Margin} \times \text{Average Lifespan}$).
3. Calculate LTV:CAC Ratio (Target $> 3$).
4. Compute Months to Recover CAC ($\text{CAC} / (\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin})$).

## Expected Output
A CLV:CAC ratio with payback period.
