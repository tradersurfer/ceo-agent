# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) Framework

## Definition
Capture the context, decision, and consequences of significant technical choices in lightweight records.

## When to Use
Any irreversible or high-impact technical decision.

## Execution Steps
1. **Title:** Define decision name and sequential ID.
2. **Context:** Document the technical drivers, business constraints, and options evaluated.
3. **Decision:** State the chosen solution clearly.
4. **Consequences:** Outline positive impacts, risks, and trade-offs.

## Expected Output
A context, decision, and consequences record.
