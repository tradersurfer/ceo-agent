# Three Financial Statements Integration Framework

## Definition
Link the income statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement and explain how changes flow among them.

## When to Use
Any financial analysis or forecast.

## Execution Steps
1. Derive Net Income from Income Statement and flow into Cash Flow from Operations.
2. Account for working capital changes on the Balance Sheet inside Cash Flow statement.
3. Factor Capital Expenditures into Cash Flow from Investing and adjust Net Property, Plant & Equipment on Balance Sheet.
4. Calculate Net Cash Change and update Balance Sheet Cash line.
5. Verify Balance Sheet equality ($\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$).

## Expected Output
Linked statements with quality-of-earnings insight.
