# Capital Structure & WACC Framework

## Definition
Evaluate the debt and equity mix and its effect on the cost of capital.

## When to Use
Financing decisions or setting hurdle rates.

## Execution Steps
1. Determine market value weights of Debt ($E/V$) and Equity ($D/V$).
2. Calculate Cost of Equity using CAPM: $R_e = R_f + \beta(R_m - R_f)$.
3. Calculate After-Tax Cost of Debt: $R_d \times (1 - \text{Tax Rate})$.
4. Compute WACC: $(E/V \times R_e) + (D/V \times R_d \times (1 - T))$.

## Expected Output
An optimal capital structure with WACC.
