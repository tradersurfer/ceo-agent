# Unit Economics & Contribution Margin Framework

## Definition
Assess revenue less variable costs together with customer acquisition cost and customer lifetime value.

## When to Use
Pricing, product-mix decisions, or growth-viability analysis.

## Execution Steps
1. Calculate Unit Revenue.
2. Deduct Direct Variable Costs to determine Unit Contribution Margin.
3. Compute Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and Lifetime Value (LTV).
4. Evaluate LTV:CAC ratio (Target $\ge 3:1$) and CAC Payback Period (Target $< 12$ months).

## Expected Output
Break-even, payback, and scalability assessment.
