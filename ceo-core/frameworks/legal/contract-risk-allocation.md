# Contract Risk Allocation Framework

## Definition
Allocate risk between parties through indemnification, limitation of liability, warranties, termination, and intellectual-property terms.

## When to Use
Reviewing or structuring an agreement before qualified-counsel sign-off.

## Execution Steps
1. Audit Indemnification terms.
2. Audit Limitation of Liability caps and exclusions.
3. Evaluate Intellectual Property ownership clauses.
4. Evaluate Termination rights and consequences.

## Expected Output
A clause-level risk-allocation summary with flagged exposures and escalation points.
