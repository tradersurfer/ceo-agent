# IRAC Legal Analysis Framework

## Definition
Analyze a legal question through Issue, Rule, Application, and Conclusion, separating the governing rule from its application to the specific facts.

## When to Use
Structured legal issue spotting or a reasoned position on a specific question.

## Execution Steps
1. **Issue:** State the specific legal question.
2. **Rule:** Identify the applicable law, regulation, or precedent.
3. **Application:** Apply the rule to the specific business facts.
4. **Conclusion:** State the legal conclusion and recommended escalation posture.

## Expected Output
A labeled issue, rule, application, and conclusion with the qualified-counsel dependency named.
