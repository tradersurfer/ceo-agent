# Enterprise Risk Management (COSO / Three Lines of Defense) Framework

## Definition
Identify, assess, own, and govern enterprise risk across the operating, oversight, and assurance lines of defense.

## When to Use
Governance-level risk assessment or assigning risk ownership across the organization.

## Execution Steps
1. Identify risks across Strategic, Operational, Financial, and Compliance categories.
2. Assign ownership across Three Lines of Defense:
   - **Line 1:** Operating management controls.
   - **Line 2:** Risk and compliance oversight.
   - **Line 3:** Independent internal audit assurance.

## Expected Output
A prioritized risk register with owners and defense-line accountability.
