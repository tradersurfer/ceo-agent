# Regulatory Compliance Mapping Framework

## Definition
Map applicable laws and regulations to concrete obligations, the controls that satisfy them, and the owner accountable for each.

## When to Use
Assessing regulatory exposure or building a compliance obligation register.

## Execution Steps
1. List statutory and regulatory requirements.
2. Translate laws into concrete business obligations.
3. Define internal operational controls.
4. Assign an accountable owner for every control.

## Expected Output
An obligation-to-control-to-owner map with identified gaps.
