# Ansoff Matrix Framework

## Definition
Evaluate growth through market penetration, market development, product development, and diversification.

## When to Use
Growth-option evaluation.

## Execution Steps
1. **Market Penetration:** Sell existing products to existing markets (lowest risk).
2. **Product Development:** Introduce new products to existing markets.
3. **Market Development:** Introduce existing products to new geographic or demographic markets.
4. **Diversification:** Introduce new products to new markets (highest risk).
5. Score each option by risk, required capital, and return.

## Expected Output
Ranked growth paths with risk assessment.
