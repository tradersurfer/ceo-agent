# McKinsey 7-S Framework

## Definition
Assess alignment among Strategy, Structure, Systems, Shared Values, Skills, Style, and Staff.

## When to Use
Organizational diagnosis or change management.

## Execution Steps
1. **Hard Elements:** Evaluate Strategy, Structure, and Systems.
2. **Soft Elements:** Evaluate Shared Values, Style, Staff, and Skills.
3. Identify misalignment gaps between current state and strategic goals across all 7 dimensions.
4. Formulate alignment plans to restore organizational balance.

## Expected Output
Alignment gaps with an intervention plan.
