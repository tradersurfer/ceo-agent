# SCARF Model Framework

## Definition
Assess how a change affects Status, Certainty, Autonomy, Relatedness, and Fairness — the social domains that drive threat or reward responses.

## When to Use
Change communication, feedback, or any people-affecting decision that could trigger a threat response.

## Execution Steps
1. Evaluate impact of proposed change across 5 social domains: **Status**, **Certainty**, **Autonomy**, **Relatedness**, **Fairness**.
2. Identify threat triggers (e.g., reduced autonomy or uncertain roles).
3. Redesign communications and rollouts to convert social threats into reward responses.

## Expected Output
A per-domain risk read with mitigations for the social response.
