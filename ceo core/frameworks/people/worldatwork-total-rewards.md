# WorldatWork Total Rewards Framework

## Definition
Frame the full employment value exchange across compensation, benefits, well-being, recognition, and development, rather than base pay alone.

## When to Use
Designing or explaining a rewards approach, or diagnosing a retention or offer problem.

## Execution Steps
1. Audit 5 total reward pillars: Compensation, Benefits, Work-Life Effectiveness, Recognition, Performance & Career Development.
2. Identify reward misalignments causing attrition.
3. Reallocate rewards budget to maximize employee perceived value.

## Expected Output
A total-rewards view identifying the levers beyond base pay.
